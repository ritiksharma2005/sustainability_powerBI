# 🌍 GHG Emission Analytics Dashboard | Power BI

## Overview

Sustainability has become a key focus area for modern industries, and understanding greenhouse gas (GHG) emissions is an essential part of achieving environmental goals.

This project presents an interactive Power BI dashboard developed to analyse and monitor Greenhouse Gas (GHG) emissions for a rubber manufacturing industry. The dashboard provides a consolidated view of Scope 1, Scope 2, and Scope 3 emissions, helping stakeholders identify major emission sources, monitor monthly trends, and support sustainability initiatives.

> **Note:** The original dataset belongs to the organization and cannot be shared due to confidentiality agreements. This repository showcases the dashboard design, business insights, and analytical approach.

---

## Business Problem

Managing greenhouse gas emissions across multiple operational activities is challenging, especially when data is scattered across different departments.

The objective of this dashboard is to:

- Monitor company-wide carbon emissions
- Compare emissions across Scope 1, Scope 2 and Scope 3
- Identify major emission contributors
- Analyse department-wise and category-wise emissions
- Track monthly emission trends
- Support ESG reporting and sustainability decision-making

## Scope 1 Emission Analysis

Scope 1 represents **direct emissions** generated from company-owned or controlled sources.

The dashboard includes:

- Fuel type analysis
- Department-wise emissions
- Source category comparison
- Quarterly distribution
- Fuel contribution percentage

Major emission sources include:

- Diesel vehicles
- DG Sets
- LPG Consumption
- Petrol vehicles

Business Insight:

The Logistics department contributes the highest Scope 1 emissions, with Diesel-owned vehicles accounting for the largest share of direct emissions.

---

## Scope 2 Emission Analysis

Scope 2 focuses on **indirect emissions** resulting from purchased electricity.

This page compares:

- Grid Electricity Consumption
- Solar Energy Consumption
- Monthly electricity usage
- Monthly CO₂ emissions

Business Insight:

Solar energy helps reduce dependence on grid electricity, supporting lower indirect emissions and improving sustainability performance.

---

## Scope 3 Emission Analysis

Scope 3 includes **indirect emissions across the value chain**, which often represent the largest portion of an organization's carbon footprint.

The dashboard analyses:

- Purchased Goods & Services
- Upstream Transportation
- Business Travel
- Employee Commuting
- Waste Generated
- Activity-wise emissions
- Department-wise emissions
- Monthly trends

Business Insight:

Upstream transportation is the largest contributor to Scope 3 emissions, indicating a significant opportunity to reduce emissions through logistics optimisation and supply chain improvements.

# Business Insights

Some key observations from the dashboard include:

- Scope 3 contributes the largest share of total greenhouse gas emissions.
- Transportation activities are the primary source of Scope 3 emissions.
- Diesel-operated vehicles account for the majority of Scope 1 emissions.
- Solar energy adoption helps reduce Scope 2 emissions.
- Department-wise analysis enables management to identify high-emission operational areas and prioritize sustainability initiatives.
